# Jetlet: Solar Escape

**Escape a dying star, one jump at a time.**

The sun is collapsing, and the only way out is up.

Jetlet: Solar Escape is a fast, one-touch arcade climber. Bounce from platform to platform, pick up speed, and push past your best line before the star pulls you back down.

## Features

- ☀ **Endless climb.** Every run is a new shot at your record.
- 🌀 **Shifting zones.** Drift, Glass, Phantom and Squish zones change the rules as you rise.
- 🏁 **Race mode.** Challenge friends on the same Wi-Fi, with no account needed.
- 🎁 **Item boxes.** Hit your rivals, block their attacks.
- 🎨 **Make it yours.** Customize your Jetlet.

Free on Google Play. How high can you get?
